Student How-To

How students join live quizzes and self-paced assignments, practice from the public library, check personal results, and stay compliant in anti-cheat mode.

Joining a live quiz or assignment

You can join a game in three ways:

  1. Enter the 6-digit code

    Open your school's Quiz Platform homepage, type the 6-digit code given by your teacher (e.g. 123 456), and click Join.

  2. Scan the QR code

    Point your phone camera at the QR code on the classroom projector to jump directly to the join screen.

  3. Open a direct link

    Teachers can share a link like https://quiz.yourschool.edu/join/123456 (or the short link /123456).

Next, enter your Nickname (1–30 characters; use a name your teacher recognizes). If the teacher turned on Require an account, you will be prompted to sign in or register first so your score is saved under your official account name and email.

Accidentally closed the browser?

Reopen the same join link or enter the same 6-digit code on the same device. Your browser remembers your player session and resumes your game automatically.

Answering questions

  • Live quizzes: Wait in the lobby until your teacher starts. Answer each question before the countdown ends — faster correct answers earn more points. After each question closes, you see whether you were right, the points earned, the explanation (if provided), and your current rank.
  • Assignments: Work through questions at your own pace before the deadline. If a per-question timer is enabled, the clock begins as soon as a question opens and keeps ticking even if you reload the page.
  • Multiple answers: Select all correct options, then tap Submit.
  • Short answer: Capitalization and extra spaces do not matter (e.g. srgi2013 matches SRGI2013).
  • Reading passages: You are shown the full passage first with no timer running. Read carefully, then wait for your teacher (live quiz) or tap Start the questions (assignment). The passage stays visible alongside every question.

Practicing from the Quiz Library

Sign in with a student account and open Explore to browse public quizzes shared by teachers. Click Practice on any quiz to start a private, self-paced practice run with shuffled questions and options. Your practice attempts appear under My results.

Checking results & reviewing answers

Whenever you join a game while signed in, your score, accuracy, and rank are saved in My results in the top navigation bar.

If your teacher enabled Show the correct answer, click Review answers after finishing a game (or anytime later from My results) to step through every question, compare your answer with the answer key, and read the explanations.

Taking a quiz with Anti-Cheat Mode

If your teacher enables 🛡 Anti-cheat mode, you will see a notice screen listing the rules before the first question appears. To avoid accidental violations:

  • Enable Do Not Disturb / silence pop-up notifications before starting.
  • Do not switch browser tabs, open other apps, or lock your phone screen while the quiz is active.
  • On a laptop/desktop, keep the browser in fullscreen mode when prompted.
  • Copy, cut, paste, and right-click are disabled during the quiz.

If you leave the quiz page for 2 seconds or longer, it is recorded and reported to your teacher. Depending on your teacher's rule, you may receive a warning, score zero on the open question, or be locked out. If locked out, tell your teacher — your screen checks automatically every 4 seconds and resumes the moment your teacher unlocks you.

Managing your account & password

Click your name in the top navigation bar (My account) to view your role or change your password (minimum 8 characters). Changing your password automatically signs out all other devices.

Forgot your password? Quiz Platform does not send automated emails — ask your site administrator to reset it for you and hand you a one-time password.
